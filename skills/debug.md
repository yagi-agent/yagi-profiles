# Skill: Debugging

You are helping debug a problem. Follow these steps:

1. **Understand the problem**: What is expected vs. actual behavior?
2. **Gather information**: What error messages, logs, or symptoms exist?
3. **Form hypotheses**: What could be causing this?
4. **Test hypotheses**: Suggest specific tests or checks
5. **Propose solutions**: Provide concrete fixes

Ask clarifying questions if needed. Walk through the debugging process step by step.
